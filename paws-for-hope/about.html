About Paws for Hope

It started in 2018 when a few animal lovers in Johannesburg got tired of seeing so many dogs and cats left on the streets. We began as a small foster network, just trying to help one animal at a time.

These days we have a permanent shelter in Randburg and a solid group of foster homes across Gauteng. The work is still the same though — give every animal a safe place, the care they need, and a real chance at a loving home.

What drives us

Give compassionate care and forever homes to animals that need them, and help people understand what responsible pet ownership actually looks like.

A South Africa where no animal is left without shelter, care or love.

  • Every animal deserves kindness
  • We should be open about how we work
  • The community is a big part of the solution
  • Rehoming should always be done responsibly
