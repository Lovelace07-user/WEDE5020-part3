Animals Looking for Forever Homes

Every animal listed here has been vaccinated and sterilised (when old enough). They’re ready for a proper home. Have a look and get in touch if one of them catches your eye — we can arrange a meet-and-greet.

Max – Male Labrador Mix

Age: 3 years | Size: Large

Max is a friendly, energetic boy who loves long walks and a good game of fetch. He’s great with kids and other dogs. Looking for an active family who will give him plenty of exercise.

Bella – Female Jack Russell Mix

Age: 2 years | Size: Small

Bella is bright, affectionate and full of personality. She’s house-trained and loves being the centre of attention. She’d do well in a home with older children.

Rocky – Male German Shepherd Mix

Age: 4 years | Size: Large

Rocky is loyal and protective. He’s done basic obedience training and would suit someone with a bit of experience who can give him structure and love.

Luna – Female Domestic Shorthair

Age: 1 year | Size: Medium

Luna is gentle and playful. She enjoys quiet evenings and would be happy in an apartment or a house with other cats.

Simba – Male Domestic Longhair

Age: 5 years | Size: Medium

Simba is a calm, affectionate senior who loves a good lap. He’s looking for a peaceful home where he can settle in and be spoiled a little.

How adoption works

  1. Browse the animals on this page
  2. Fill in an enquiry form or get in touch
  3. Come meet the animal at the shelter
  4. We do a quick home check
  5. If everything feels right, you take your new family member home
