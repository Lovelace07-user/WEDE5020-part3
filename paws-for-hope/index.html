We’ve been rescuing, rehabilitating and finding new homes for abandoned dogs and cats across Gauteng since 2018.

Hi, welcome to Paws for Hope

We’re a small non-profit based in Randburg. Every day we take in dogs and cats that have been abandoned, abused or left to fend for themselves. We get them the medical care they need, give them time to recover, and then work hard to find them proper forever homes.

None of this would be possible without our volunteers, foster families and the people who support us. If you’ve ever thought about helping, we’d love to hear from you.
